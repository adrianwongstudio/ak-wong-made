## Project title
Toastmasters District Dashboard *(rename as you like)*

## Teaser
The Toastmasters District dashboard doesn't display club progress toward the
Distinguished Club Program in a visual way. I was a District Leader in the
past, so I knew the pain first-hand. Built a dedicated dashboard that
updates twice monthly and makes club standing readable at a glance.
