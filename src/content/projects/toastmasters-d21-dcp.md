    caption: District Dashboard — Toastmasters International

      The official district dashboard doesn't display a club's points toward the
      Distinguished Club Program in any visual way. It's not the most readable at
      a glance. I was a District Leader in the past, so I knew the pain first-hand.


      [dashboards.toastmasters.org/District.aspx?id=21&hideclub=1](https://dashboards.toastmasters.org/District.aspx?id=21&hideclub=1)

    caption: Club Dashboard — Toastmasters International

      District leaders had to click into every single club to see its score. Fine
      for one club at a time, terrible for reviewing an entire district on a call.


      [dashboards.toastmasters.org/ClubReport.aspx?id=05265003](https://dashboards.toastmasters.org/ClubReport.aspx?id=05265003)

  - chapterTitle: What was built
    image: /media/toastmasters/03-current-year-table.png
    caption: The current year — every club in the district
    body: >
      A single scannable table showing every club's progress on all ten Distinguished
      Club Program goals. Filled squares are goals achieved, outlined are still
      reachable, struck-through are windows that have closed. Sort by any column,
      filter by division. Updates on the 1st and 15th of every month.

  - image: /media/toastmasters/04-club-detail-view.png
    caption: Per-club detail — five-year trace
    body: >
      Click into any club for a five-year history of every goal, plus the current
      year's status at a glance. District leaders can now review a club with the
      club's leadership on a call without having to open half a dozen tabs. Also
      exports to Excel for follow-up conversations.

  - image: /media/toastmasters/05-climbed-and-slipped.png
    caption: Clubs that climbed, clubs that slipped — year over year
    body: >
      Year-over-year comparison surfaces where to spend leadership energy. Clubs
      that slipped need help — district leaders can call them, offer training, or
      pair them with mentor clubs. Clubs that climbed need recognition and are
      good models to point others toward.

  - image: /media/toastmasters/06-year-by-year.png
    caption: Every club, year by year — five-year retrospective
    body: >
      Five years of year-end scores per club, with a mini sparkline to spot trends
      quickly. Search by club name or number to open up any club's full history.
      Sorted by name, division, or trajectory.
