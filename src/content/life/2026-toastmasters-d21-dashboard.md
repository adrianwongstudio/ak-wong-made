I was a Toastmasters District Leader, so I knew the official dashboard
hides how clubs are doing on the Distinguished Club Program. You had to
open each club one at a time. I built a single page that shows every club
in District 21 at once, with five years of history and an Excel export. It
updates on the 1st and 15th of each month.
